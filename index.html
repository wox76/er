MISSION

EraDrive Autonomy

EraDrive provides a unified, single-source platform for space safety, performance, and defense. We streamline the complexities of the modern orbital environment by seamlessly integrating space traffic management, on-orbit logistics, and space situational awareness into a cohesive ecosystem.

Driven by our AI-native architecture, our platform handles everything from deep learning perception and real-time trajectory optimization to resilient alternative positioning and automated collision avoidance. This enables our partners to focus entirely on their primary missions while actively participating in a real-time, global space traffic intelligence network.

CORE CAPABILITIES

Why Choose Us?

THE PROBLEM

Three Pressing Problems in Spaceflight

Legacy space infrastructure faces critical barriers across security, performance, and traffic management.

01

Space is Congested

Outdated, ground-based, fragmented space traffic management systems put orbit safety at risk.

How do we detect uncooperative space objects and react to them?

02

Space is Scaling

Outdated, rule-based, micromanaged, human-controlled systems are not able to meet requirements.

How to enable fully autonomous, multi-satellite prox ops and formation flight?

03

Space is Contested

Legacy technology is not ready to counteract adversarial threats, to provide surprise or offense.

How do we navigate above the GPS constellation and when RF is jammed?
